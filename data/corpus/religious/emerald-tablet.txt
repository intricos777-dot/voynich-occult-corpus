THE EMERALD TABLET OF HERMES TRISMEGISTUS

Truth is certain, truth is that which is.
That which is above is like that which is below.
And that which is below is like that which is above.
To make the miracle of the One thing.

As all things were made from the One,
By the One,
So all things were made from this One thing,
By adaptation.

Its father is the Sun,
Its mother is the Moon.

The Wind carried it in its belly,
The Earth is its nurse.

It is the father of all perfection in the whole world.

Its power is integrating if it be turned into Earth.

Separate the Earth from the Fire,
The subtle from the gross,
With great judgment and skill.

It ascends from Earth to Heaven,
And descends again to Earth,
And receives the power of the Above and the Below.

By this means you shall have the glory of the whole world.
And all obscurity shall flee from you.

This is the strong force of all forces,
Overcoming every subtle thing,
And penetrating every solid thing.

As the world was created,
The marvellous things are from this one thing.
