TAO TE CHING

by Lao Tzu, translated by Stephen Mitchell

1. The tao that can be told
is not the eternal Tao.
The name that can be named
is not the eternal Name.

2. The unnamable is the eternally real.
Naming is the origin
of all particular things.

3. Free from desire, you realize the mystery.
Caught in desire, you see only manifestations.

4. Yet mystery and manifestations
arise from the same source.
This source is called darkness.

5. Darkness within darkness.
The gateway to all understanding.

6. The Tao is like a well:
used but never used up.
It is like the eternal void:
filled with infinite possibilities.

7. It is hidden but always present.
I don't know who gave birth to it.
It is older than God.

8. The Tao is infinite, unborn, and unchanging.
It is the mother of the ten thousand things.

9. Fill your bowl to the brim
and it will spill.
Keep sharpening your knife
and it will blunt.

10. Chase after money and security
and your heart will never unclench.

11. Care about people's approval
and you will be their prisoner.

12. Do your work, then step back.
The only path to serenity.

13. The Master leads
by emptying people's minds
and filling their cores,
by weakening their ambition
and toughening their resolve.

14. The Tao is called the Great Vault:
the treasury of the ten thousand things.

15. Endless source of creation,
it does not drain or overflow.

16. It does not reject anyone,
and does not demand worship.
