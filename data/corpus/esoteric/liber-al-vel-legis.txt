CHAPTER I

1. Had! The manifestation of Nuit.
2. The unveiling of the company of heaven.
3. Every man and every woman is a star.
4. Every number is infinite; there is no difference.
5. Help me, o warrior lord of Thebes, in my unveiling before the Children of men!

21. Slay fourth the love of children!
22. Slay fifth the love of animals!
23. Slay sixth the love of science!
24. I am Nuit, and my word is six and fifty.
25. Divide, add, multiply, and understand.
26. Then saith the prophet and slave of the beauteous one: Who am I, and what is to be the sign?
27. So she answered him: We all have our garments of light, and each is but a star.
28. But thou art not as yet a star.
29. Thou art but a man, and the slave of the beauteous one.
30. Therefore let thy work be to make the star rise within thee.
31. For I am divided for love's sake, for the chance of union.
32. This is the creation of the world, that the pain of division is as nothing, and the joy of dissolution all.
33. For these fools of men and their woes care not thou at all!
34. They feel little; what is, is balanced by weak & eager pains.
35. But ye are not so chosen.
36. We are glad to be glad!
37. Worship the Khabs, and behold my light shed over you!
38. There is no law beyond Do what thou wilt.
39. The word of the Law is Thelema.
40. Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.
41. The word of the sin is restriction.
42. O man! refuse no thy wife, if she will!
43. O lover, if thou wilt, depart!
44. There is no bond that can unite the divided but love.
45. Give heed unto my message!
46. Nothing is a secret key of this law. Sixty-one the Jews call it; I call it eight, eighty, four hundred & eighteen.
47. But they have the half: unite by thine art so that all disappear.
48. My prophet is a fool with his one, one, one; are not they the Ox, and none by the Book?
49. Abrogate are all rituals, all ordeals, all words and signs.
50. Ra-Hoor-Khuit hath taken his seat in the East at the Equinox of the Gods.
51. Let Asar be with Isa, who also are one.
52. But they are not of me. Let Asar be the adorant, Isa the sufferer.
53. Hoor in his splendour and his glory is hidden.
54. There is a light before thine eyes, o prophet, a single light, the light of a star.
55. And this light is also thy light, and it is in thee and about thee.
56. And the earth shall be glad thereof.
57. Invoke me under my stars! Love is the law, love under will.
58. Nor let the fools mistake love; for there are love and love.
59. There is the dove, and there is the serpent.
60. Choose ye well!
61. My number is 11, as all their numbers who are of us.
62. Let the daughter of the horse-leech say: How excellent is my sucking!
63. Be not caught by the morning-glory, nor by theBind-weed, nor by the night-shade, nor by the oak, nor by the yew.
64. Look on my star, and the giver of the death of the Saviour!
65. To me! To me!
66. The Manifestation of Nuit is at an end.
